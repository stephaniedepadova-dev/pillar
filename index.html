FIELD WORK · SOURCING HYPOTHESIS

Three talent pools. Different strengths.

For a commercial role serving construction SMEs, I would test adjacent markets rather than search only for people with the same job title.

POOL 01 · CLOSEST FIT

Construction software sellers

Account Executives and area managers at construction or technical software vendors. They already understand contractors and operational workflows.

Market examples to investigate: TeamSystem · ACCA software
POOL 02 · ADJACENT MARKET

Building materials sales

Technical sales representatives who visit job sites and build trusted relationships with construction businesses. Strong sector proximity; SaaS capability needs assessment.

Market examples to investigate: Mapei · Kerakoll
POOL 03 · TRANSFERABLE MOTION

SMB SaaS and fintech

Full-cycle sellers used to consultative sales, prospecting and objections from small-business decision-makers. Construction knowledge needs validation.

Market examples to investigate: Qonto · Satispay

("account executive" OR "sales executive" OR "area manager" OR "business developer" OR "key account") AND (edilizia OR costruzioni OR construction OR cantiere OR "imprese edili" OR "materiali edili") AND (SaaS OR software OR gestionale OR fintech OR digitale) NOT (stage OR intern OR tirocinio OR junior)

09 /QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

CLEAR ANSWERS FOR PEOPLE AND SEARCH ENGINES

Talent Acquisition,
without the jargon.

What does a Talent Acquisition Specialist do?

A Talent Acquisition Specialist manages hiring end to end: understands business needs, defines role profiles, sources and screens candidates, coordinates interviews, supports offers and measures recruitment performance.

What is technical recruitment?

Technical recruitment identifies and assesses candidates for specialised roles in software, IT, engineering, embedded systems and digital technologies.

How do you build an effective recruitment funnel?

Start with a role intake and scorecard, map the talent market, activate sourcing, qualify candidates, coordinate structured interviews, manage offers and review conversion and time-to-feedback at every stage.

Where is Stephanie De Padova based?

Stephanie De Padova is based in Turin, Italy, and is open to opportunities in Turin, Milan and remote roles across Italy.

Does Stephanie have B2B SaaS recruitment experience?

Her established recruitment experience is in IT and Engineering. B2B SaaS is a transferable growth area: she brings full-cycle recruiting, sourcing, stakeholder management and digital strategy skills, while being transparent about the sector-specific learning curve.
